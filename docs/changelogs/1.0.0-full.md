# Scribe 1.0.0 — Full changelog (since 0.2.0)

Scribe's first "complete" cut. The new **Tablets** tier fills in the earliest end of the progression, giving a full handheld → carried → placed writing system, all feeding the per-player pinned-task HUD.

---

## Added

### Tablets — a new early-game writing tier

- **Clay & Wax Tablets.** A cheap handheld tablet craftable long before a Notebook or Lectern, holding a short list (up to **10 tasks and 1 pinned task**) as a quick scratchpad. Grid-crafted from a knife + stick + clay (or a saw + planks + stick + beeswax for the wax variant) — no writing set or fired ink bowl required. Each has an in-game handbook entry.
- **Clay life-cycle: dry, re-wet, and fire.** A freshly crafted clay Tablet is **wet** and freely editable. Left alone it dries **hard** over ~2 in-game days, locking its writing. From there you can **dunk it in water** (drop it into a water block, or Shift+right-click aimed at water) to soften it back to wet — resetting its drying timer — or **fire it in a fire pit**, like pottery, to make its writing **permanent** (water no longer softens it). Firing a blank tablet sets its empty surface in stone.
- **Clay-type tablets** — Red, Blue, and Fire Clay. The colour is cosmetic (all three behave identically), each with its own crafting recipe and a distinct full-page dialog backdrop, and — once fired — a distinct fired-ceramic appearance.
- **Wax Tablet** — a reusable step up: a wooden frame filled with beeswax that **never dries or fires**, so it can always be rewritten. Same 10-task / 1-pin limits as clay. Balanced against clay by material cost and by having no permanent-archive path.
- **Cuneiform script** — Tablet text is written in a bespoke carved-wedge **cuneiform** font by default. Toggle **Cuneiform tablets** off in Scribe Settings to render it in your selected task font instead. An optional **Cuneiform press-in** effect carves newly-typed letters in stroke-by-stroke (off by default; developed via a ghost lead-in that was recast to a stroke-count timing model).

### Display & storage

- **Shelf, bookshelf, and cabinet placement** for Scribe items — Tablets and Notebooks can be placed on vanilla storage shelving. Per-material display transforms were tuned per surface (clay vs. wax differ in scale/offset) and de-duplicated onto the shape base. *(Commits `e4e7cbf`, `50f92f6`, `8c331b3`, `fcbfa96`.)*

### Interaction

- **Quick-add task (Shift+right-click)** — Shift+right-clicking any Scribe surface (Lectern, Notebook, Clockmaker's Notebook, or a wet Tablet) opens its editor with a fresh empty task already added at the **top** of the list and the caret focused. A quick-add at a full tablet (10-task cap) opens the editor and shows the "tablet is full" notice without adding a row. On a hard or fired (read-only) tablet it simply opens the tablet.
- **In-game error notices** — a refused edit now tells you *why* through the vanilla client error channel instead of failing silently (e.g. "A tablet holds at most 10 tasks", a locked hardened/fired tablet, or a held Lectern edit lock).

### Crafting

- **Clockmaker's Notebook Schematic** — a reusable blueprint sold occasionally by wandering **Commodities** and **Treasure Hunter** traders. Added to the grid alongside the usual Notebook + temporal gear + metal parts, it lets **any** player craft a Clockmaker's Notebook without the Clockmaker class or Tinkerer trait. Not consumed, so one purchase lasts forever. The existing trait-gated recipe and the `scribeClockmakerRequiresTrait` world setting are unchanged — this is an additional path, not a replacement.

## Changed

- **BREAKING — right-click modifier gestures changed.** With quick-add taking Shift+right-click, two older gestures moved:
  - **Lectern:** Shift+right-click no longer opens the plain editor — it now quick-adds. To open the editor without adding a task, right-click to Read, then use the **Task Editor** nav tab.
  - **Notebook, Clockmaker's Notebook, and Tablet:** placing the held item on the ground now requires **Ctrl+Shift+right-click** (vanilla spear convention). The Tablet's water-soften gesture is unchanged — Shift+right-click **aimed at water** still softens a hard tablet; only Shift+right-click *not* aimed at water changed.
- The editor footer's Information (ⓘ) button now **toggles** the "Scribe Editor Features" handbook page (open when closed; close when already open; navigate-then-close if open on another page). Tooltip now reads "Show / hide Editor Features". Shared across the Lectern, both Notebooks, and the tablet editors.
- **Localization sweep** — the last hardcoded UI strings were routed through `Lang` (V.4).

## Fixed

- **First-open dialog flicker.** The **first** right-click on a not-yet-crafted Scribe item (Notebook, Clockmaker's Notebook, or Tablet) no longer flickers its dialog closed and requires a second click. The one-time server "picked up" history sync was being misread as a switch to a different item; fixed via a presence-only re-sync guard.
- **Settings numeric arrow-key stepping.** Stepping a Scribe Settings numeric field with Up/Down now works on **every** consecutive press. Previously, with a document editor also open, the arrow keys leaked to the editor's last-touched row after the first step (driving that row's caret) because the open editor kept capturing keyboard input; the editor now only captures input while it is the focused dialog.
- **Wax-tablet writing bleeding through when placed.** A placed/shelved wax tablet let the writing show through the tablet body to whatever was behind it. Root cause: stray sub-255 alpha pixels in the body texture demoted the whole mesh into the WBOIT transparent pass. Fixed by baking the writing into the opaque body texture (removing the semi-transparent overlay element) and flattening the body alpha to 255. *(Commit `e95585d`.)*
