# Scribe 1.0.0 — What's new since 0.2.0

Scribe's first "complete" cut: the new **Tablets** tier fills in the earliest end of the progression, so you now have a full handheld → carried → placed writing system.

- **Clay & Wax Tablets — a new stone-age writing tier.** A cheap handheld scratchpad (up to 10 tasks, 1 pin) craftable long before a Notebook or Lectern.
- **Clay life-cycle: dry → re-wet → fire.** A fresh clay tablet is wet and editable; left alone it **dries hard** (locking the writing) over ~2 in-game days. **Dunk it in water** to soften it back and revise, or **fire it in a firepit** to make the writing permanent.
- **Wax Tablet** — a reusable step up that never dries or fires, so it's always rewritable.
- **Clay colours** — Red, Blue, and Fire Clay (cosmetic; each with its own recipe and fired look).
- **Cuneiform script** — tablet text is carved in a wedge-script font by default, with a plain-text toggle in Scribe Settings.
- **Display on shelves, bookshelves, and cabinets** — Tablets and Notebooks can now be placed on vanilla storage shelving for decoration and organization.
- **Quick-add task** — Shift + right-click any Scribe item to open it straight to a fresh, focused task at the top of the list.
- **⚠️ Breaking gesture change** — because Shift + right-click now quick-adds, **placing a held Notebook/Tablet on the ground moved to Ctrl + Shift + right-click** (vanilla spear convention). Shift + right-click aimed at water still softens a hard clay tablet.
- **Clockmaker's Notebook Schematic** — a trader-sold blueprint that lets anyone craft a Clockmaker's Notebook without the Clockmaker class.
- **In-game error notices** — refused edits (tablet full, locked, editor held) now say why instead of failing silently.
- **Fixes** — first-click dialog flicker on uncrafted items; Settings arrow-key stepping leaking to the editor; wax-tablet writing showing through the tablet when placed.

Requires **LibGUI (gui) 3.1.0** on both client and server. Existing worlds load clean (additive).
