⚠️ Requires LibGUI (gui mod 3.1.0) — Scribe won't load without it. Install LibGUI from the mod DB first, on both client and server.
Vintage Story is an epic journey — and a logistical nightmare. You log off mid-project, trek 3,000 blocks for ore, and by the time you return, that vital mental note to 'smelt bronze before winter' has completely evaporated somewhere between baking pies and clayforming.

Scribe is my attempt to bridge that gap — a mod for keeping your goals inside the world, instead of on a sticky note next to your monitor.

📖 What you get
	•	Clay & Wax Tablets (NEW in v1.0) — the earliest, cheapest writing surface, craftable in the stone age long before a Notebook or Lectern. A quick handheld scratchpad (up to 10 tasks, 1 pin). A fresh clay tablet is wet and editable; left alone it dries hard over a couple of in-game days, locking the writing — then dunk it in water to soften it back and revise, or fire it in a fire pit to make the writing permanent. The Wax Tablet is a reusable step up that never dries or fires. Tablet text is written in a carved-wedge cuneiform script by default (toggle to plain text in Settings). Clay comes in red, blue, and fire-clay colours.
	•	Lectern block — a craftable, placeable notebook you write directly on. Task checklist + freeform note section. Multiplayer-safe: edits sync live, one person edits at a time while everyone else reads. Drop one outside your base and its Guest Book tab records visitors, who can leave a note.
	•	Notebook — a carried, craftable item you write in anywhere. Same task checklist + notes as the Lectern, but personal and portable: it's yours, it travels with you, and it quietly keeps a History chronicle of what happened while you carried it (crafted, picked up, deaths, PvP and boss kills, temporal storms).
	•	Clockmaker's Notebook — the Notebook's advanced sibling, adding a Timer tab for real-time and in-game-time reminders that fire a toast and sound (optionally alongside your pinned tasks). Crafted from a Notebook + a temporal gear + metal parts. Its craft is gated to the Clockmaker character class (the tinkerer trait) by default; a server can lift that with /worldconfig scribeClockmakerRequiresTrait false (takes effect on restart), or anyone can craft it with the trader-sold Schematic.
	•	Scriptorium — a dedicated shared writing station, crafted like a more-expensive Lectern (same writing kit, eight planks). Drop a Scribe item in and transcribe it onto another — duplicate a checklist, or append one document onto another — with a stamp flourish and sound. The same tab round-trips a document as JSON (exact) or TSV (spreadsheet-friendly).
	•	Chalkboard (NEW in v1.3) — the Lectern hung on a wall like a painting. Same shared document, Guest Book, and editor lock; capped at 10 tasks. Point at a wall to mount it. Not a drawable board — you write tasks on it the same way you write on a Lectern.
	•	Crafting Tasks (NEW in v1.3) — from an item's Handbook page, Add Crafting Task binds a grid recipe: the row tracks the output and builds an ingredient shopping list underneath (liquids as litre trackers). Items with several recipes get a labeled link per variant. Item Trackers and Links live in the same "Add to Scribe" footer; cooked-meal pages offer Add Link.
	•	Pinned Task HUD — hover any task, click the pin icon, and it appears on an always-on overlay visible while you play. Your pins are yours — pinning doesn't affect other players on the same Lectern.
	•	Pin Tab — a dedicated view that lists all your pinned tasks across every Scribe item and Lectern in the world, editable in place.
	•	Scribe Settings — all preferences in one panel: pixel-art notebook theme, window size, HUD anchor and size, completion policy, font selector, and a UI sound mute. Everything updates live.
	•	Font selector — pick the typeface for your task and note text: Scapholène, Caudex, La Belle Aurore, Noto Sans, Noto Serif, Playfair Display, or Cormorant Unicase.

🔧 Getting started
	1	Install the required mod: LibGUI from the mod DB (hard dependency — don't skip this).
	2	In game, craft a Clay Tablet — the fastest way in, no writing set or fired ink needed. A knife, a stick, and six clay — red, blue, or fire clay; pick one colour. The knife isn't consumed (it just takes a little durability). Crafts one wet, ready-to-write tablet. Prefer something reusable? The Wax Tablet uses a saw, planks, a stick, and beeswax, and never dries or fires. [image: https://i.imgur.com/kZPeJ7V.png]
	3	Hold the tablet and right-click to open it, then type your tasks straight in. Shift + right-click quick-adds a task. Leave a clay tablet alone and it dries hard, locking the writing — dunk it in water to soften it back, or fire it in a fire pit to keep it forever.
	4	Hover a task row → click the pin icon → task appears on your HUD immediately.
	5	Ready for more room? Craft a Notebook to carry a full checklist and notes anywhere, or place a Lectern to write on and share with others.
For crafting recipes, keyboard shortcuts, completion policies, and the full settings reference, see the Scribe wiki on GitHub.

⚠️ Updating from v0.2? The right-click gestures changed. Shift + right-click now QUICK-ADDS a task (on the Lectern, Notebook, and wet Tablets) instead of opening the plain editor. To place a held Notebook/Tablet on the ground, use Ctrl + Shift + right-click (the vanilla spear convention). Shift + right-click aimed at water still softens a hardened clay tablet.

📌 The Pinned Task HUD
Pinned tasks show as a compact, glow-outlined list over the game world — no background plate, just readable text wherever you've anchored it. It defaults to the top-right corner with enough clearance for the minimap (and automatically removes that clearance if you've hidden the minimap).
Checking a task off from the HUD starts a 1.5-second undo window — the text fades as a countdown. Uncheck within the window to fully cancel (nothing is sent to the server). When the window elapses, your chosen completion policy fires: keep it pinned, keep and sink to bottom, unpin, unpin and sink to bottom, or delete the task from the document entirely.
Press P (rebindable under Controls → Scribe) or click the ▾ header to collapse the HUD to just its header. Position, size, row cap, and text scale are all configurable in Scribe Settings.

⚙️ Scribe Settings
Open from the gear icon in the Lectern sidebar or the gear on the HUD header — same panel either way. Notable options:
	•	Pixel-Art Display — toggles the notebook backdrop art and Scribe's parchment theme on/off. Off = your global game GUI theme.
	•	Pixel Art Size — the Lectern window width. Scales the whole layout live.
	•	Task text font — font selector for task and note row text.
	•	HUD position — pick any corner or mid-edge anchor, nudge with X/Y offsets.
	•	Mute Scribe UI sounds — silences Scribe's own button clicks without affecting the rest of the game.

🗺️ Roadmap
Note-taking tools grow with your tech tree — scratchy clay in the stone age, carried books and placed lecterns in the metal age, shared boards later. v1.0 completes that early-to-mid arc.
	•	✅ v0.1 — Lectern + Pinned HUD (released)
	•	✅ v0.2 — Notebook & Clockmaker's Notebook — a carried item for personal notes with a passive History chronicle; the Clockmaker's variant adds timers and reminders (released)
	•	✅ v1.0 — Clay & Wax Tablets — the early-game handheld tier, with the dry/harden/re-wet/fire life-cycle and a cuneiform script; Scribe's first "complete" cut (released)
	•	✅ v1.1 — Interim polish — light-aware pages, plain notes alongside tasks, shelf storage for Notebooks and Tablets, typed arrows, smooth list animations, and a batch of editor and HUD fixes (released)
	•	✅ v1.2 — Scriptorium + Tracker/Link + Transcribe — a shared writing station, item-bound task types, and copy/import/export (released)
	•	✅ v1.3.0 — Crafting Tasks + Chalkboard — recipe-bound shopping lists and a wall-hung Lectern (released)
	•	⬜ Later — Bulletin Board — a shared, public board for multiplayer
