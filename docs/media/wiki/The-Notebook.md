# The Notebook

The Notebook is a carried, personal document — the same task checklist and freeform notes as the [Lectern](Using-the-Lectern), but in your inventory instead of on a placed block.

## How it differs from the Lectern

| | Lectern | Notebook |
|---|---------|----------|
| Where it lives | A placed block in the world | An item in your inventory |
| Who can read/edit it | Shared — anyone who can reach it | Personal — whoever holds it |
| Editor lock | Yes, one editor at a time | No lock (a held stack has one holder) |
| Guestbook | Yes | — |
| History chronicle | — | Yes |

## Opening

Right-click while the Notebook is your active hand item. It opens directly in Read view; the sidebar has **Read**, **Edit** (Task Editor), **Pinned**, **History**, and **Settings**. There is no Guestbook (that's a Lectern feature).

Because a Notebook has no editor lock, you can edit it freely and immediately — there's nothing to request and nothing to release. You can switch your active hotbar slot and back without the dialog closing on you.

## Tasks, notes, and pins

Tasks and notes work exactly as they do on the Lectern, and the editor keyboard shortcuts are identical — see [Editor Reference](Editor-Reference). Tasks you pin from a Notebook appear on the same [Pinned Task HUD](Pinned-Task-HUD) as your Lectern pins; the Pinned view lists all of them together across every source.

## History

Every Notebook quietly keeps a **History** chronicle — an append-only log of notable events that happened while you carried it: it was crafted, a player picked it up, you or others died, PvP and boss kills, and temporal storms. See [History & Guestbook](History-and-Guestbook) for the full list and how it's recorded.

## Crafting

See [Crafting → Notebook](Crafting-the-Lectern#notebook). The Notebook uses the same writing-set ingredients as the Lectern (feather, parchment, leather, nails, and a fired bowl of black dye) on a 3×2 grid.
