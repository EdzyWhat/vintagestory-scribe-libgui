# Clay & Wax Tablets

Tablets are Scribe's **earliest** writing surface — cheap, handheld, and craftable long before you have the leather, parchment, and fired ink bowl a [Notebook](The-Notebook) or [Lectern](Using-the-Lectern) needs. They're grounded in the real archaeology of writing: scratch a short list into wet clay, let it dry, and fire it to keep it forever.

A Tablet is deliberately limited: **up to 10 tasks and one pinned task**. It's a quick scratchpad, not a full journal — when you outgrow it, step up to a Notebook.

## The clay life-cycle

A clay Tablet moves through three states:

| State | Editable? | How you get there |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Wet** | Yes — write and edit freely | Freshly crafted |
| **Hardened** | No — writing is locked | Left alone, a wet Tablet dries hard over **about two in-game days** |
| **Fired** | No — writing is permanent | Fire a wet or hardened Tablet in a **fire pit**, like pottery |

The two locked states differ in one important way:

- A **hardened** Tablet can be **softened back to wet** — dunk it in water (drop it into a water block, or wade in while holding it) and it becomes editable again, its drying timer reset.
- A **fired** Tablet is permanent. Water won't soften it. Fire a Tablet when you want a list preserved for good.

So the natural flow is: **write while wet → let it harden → either re-wet to revise, or fire to keep.**

## The Wax Tablet

The Wax Tablet is a small step up from clay: a wooden frame filled with beeswax. It **never dries or fires**, so it can always be rewritten — no life-cycle to manage. It shares the same limits as the clay Tablet (10 tasks, one pin).

## Opening and writing

Hold a Tablet as your active hand item, then:

- **Right-click** — open the Tablet's tasks and notes.
- **Shift + right-click** — quick-add a new task at the top without opening the full dialog.

Aiming at water while holding a **hardened** clay Tablet and right-clicking will **soften** it instead of opening it (the same gesture that re-wets it).

Tasks, notes, and pins work as they do everywhere else in Scribe — see the [Editor Reference](Editor-Reference). A task you pin from a Tablet appears on the same [Pinned Task HUD](Pinned-Task-HUD) as your Lectern and Notebook pins.

## Cuneiform script

By default, Tablet text is rendered in a carved-wedge **cuneiform** script, in keeping with the tier's ancient-writing theme. If you'd rather read it plainly, turn off **Cuneiform tablets** in [Scribe Settings](Scribe-Settings) and Tablet text renders in your selected task font instead.

## Clay colours

Clay Tablets come in three colours depending on the clay you craft them from — **red**, **blue**, and **fire clay**. The colour is purely cosmetic; all three behave identically and each carries its own dialog backdrop tint.

## Crafting

See [Crafting → Tablets](Crafting-the-Lectern#tablets). Clay Tablets are knapped from a knife, a stick, and clay; the Wax Tablet uses a saw, planks, a stick, and beeswax. Both are ungated — no character class required — and available from the very early game.
