## Why

The Clockmaker Notebook timer fires silently — the HUD flashes but there is no audio cue, so a player who isn't watching the screen misses the alarm entirely. Adding a mechanical bell sound completes the feature and makes the Clockmaker's Notebook feel like a real clockwork object.

## What Changes

- A mechanical alarm clock sound (`assets/scribe/sounds/alarm/clockbell.ogg`, Freesound #78562, CC BY 4.0, ~26.4s) is bundled with the mod.
- A new `ScribeAlarmSound` controller class manages the sound's full lifetime: eased ramp-up, sine-wave breathing, and eased fade-out on early dismiss.
- `ScribeModSystem` starts the alarm when the timer transitions to Fired and exposes a `DismissAlarm()` method.
- The HUD fired-timer click path and the Clockmaker dialog Stop Timer button both call `DismissAlarm()`.
- Attribution for Freesound #78562 (klankbibliotheek, CC BY 4.0) is added to `modinfo.json` and a `CREDITS.txt` at mod root.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `clockmaker-alarm-sound`: A client-side sound controller that plays the mechanical alarm OGG when the Clockmaker Notebook timer fires, with eased ramp-up (easeInCubic, 1s), sine-wave volume breathing (±10%, 3s period), and eased fade-out on early dismiss (easeInOutSine, 500ms). The clip plays once (no loop); it naturally ends at ~26.4s before the 30s `FiredAutoClearSeconds` window. Peak volume is calibrated to roughly match a bear growl heard at 10 blocks.

### Modified Capabilities

- `timer-lifecycle`: The dismiss signal (player clicking the HUD fired row or pressing Stop Timer) now also triggers `DismissAlarm()` in addition to clearing the timer state.

## Impact

- **New file:** `src/Mod/ScribeAlarmSound.cs` — the sound controller.
- **Modified:** `src/Mod/ScribeModSystem.cs` (or its `.Timer` partial) — creates/disposes `_activeAlarm` on `MyTimerChanged`.
- **Modified:** `src/Mod/HudScribePins.cs` — fired-timer click calls `DismissAlarm()`.
- **Modified:** `src/Mod/GuiDialogClockmakerNotebook.cs` — Stop Timer button calls `DismissAlarm()`.
- **Modified:** `modinfo.json` — attribution credit appended to description.
- **New file:** `CREDITS.txt` — full attribution for Freesound #78562.
- **New asset:** `assets/scribe/sounds/alarm/clockbell.ogg` — prepared by the author (download + normalize + export; not generated by code).
- No new mod dependencies; `ILoadedSound` is vanilla VintagestoryAPI.
- No Core changes; sound is entirely client-side Mod-layer.
- No server-side logic; `DismissAlarm()` is a client method.
